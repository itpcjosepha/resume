# Ocean Direct LLC | https://www.profoodsolutions.com/our-companies/richmond-wholesale
Senior Software Engineer / Enterprise Applications Developer

## Business Overview

Ocean Direct LLC specialized in food procurement, consolidation, and distribution services supporting both commercial and government customers, including civil and military supply chains.

The organization required a highly customized operational platform capable of managing complex product sourcing, inventory control, affiliate relationships, fulfillment processes, logistics operations, and financial reporting.

This engagement focused on modernizing and expanding an existing FileMaker-based business platform to support growing operational complexity and evolving business requirements.

---

## Project Summary

Served as lead developer responsible for enhancing, modernizing, and expanding a mission-critical business application supporting procurement, inventory management, fulfillment, logistics, accounting, and reporting operations.

The platform functioned as a centralized operational system used to manage product acquisition, inventory movement, affiliate relationships, shipment tracking, invoicing, and financial integrations.

A significant portion of the engagement involved reverse engineering existing business logic and legacy code in order to extend system capabilities while maintaining operational continuity.
