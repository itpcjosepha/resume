# Baldwin Park Unified School District (BPUSD)
Application Developer / FileMaker Consultant  
Baldwin Park, California | 2011

Website: https://www.bpusd.net

## Business Overview

Baldwin Park Unified School District serves students and families throughout the San Gabriel Valley and operates a variety of educational programs, including its Early Childhood Education and Head Start initiatives. The Head Start program focuses on supporting early childhood development, school readiness, family engagement, and student wellness. (bpusd.net)

This engagement focused on developing a custom application to support the operational and administrative requirements of the district's Head Start program.

---

## Project Summary

Designed and developed a FileMaker-based application used to manage student enrollment, program participation, classroom information, and child health-related records for the Head Start program.

The solution centralized information that had traditionally been managed through paper forms and manual processes, providing staff with a more efficient method of tracking student information and program activities.
